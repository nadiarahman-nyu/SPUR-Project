Prototype, not a live company data feed. Sample numbers are fictional. The browser does not scrape Google, TikTok, SEC filings or other sites; CSVs are processed locally and not uploaded. Public search/social signals must be entered by a researcher and checked for comparability; use official APIs and their terms if building a production integration. Non-US brands may publish annual reports or Form 20-F rather than a 10-K. Price pressure is directional context, not a selling-price forecast. Never use a forecast to justify unsafe overtime, unfair pay or impossible supplier lead times.
Method: a transparent baseline combines latest-period sales with clipped historical growth (25% weight), search-interest change (20%) and social-interest change (10%), then shows a ±20% planning range. This is a classroom prototype, not a validated machine-learning model. The sustainability framing is informed by IDEO’s H&M inventory case study and H&M’s climate reporting. A forecast alone does not demonstrate reduced production, waste or emissions.
